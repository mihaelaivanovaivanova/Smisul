# 14_Offer_and_Pricing

## Purpose

Define the launch offer, pricing structure, unit economics, and CAC limits for the first 30-day Miswak validation test in Bulgaria.

The launch is intentionally lean:
- Initial stock: **200 Miswak sticks**
- Paid advertising budget: **€400–€500 for the month**
- First major reorder decision: **after the first 7 days**
- Replenishment lead time: **~20 days**
- Primary goal: **validate whether the product can acquire cold Bulgarian customers at sustainable economics**
- Month-one financial goal: **at minimum, avoid operating at a loss**

---

## Core Pricing

### Price of 1 Miswak

**€3.99**

The single-stick option acts primarily as a low-friction entry price and price anchor. It should not be the main paid-advertising offer because its contribution margin cannot support a high CAC.

---

## Bundle options

| Bundle | Selling price | Effective price / stick | Saving vs. single price | Role |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1 Miswak | **€3.99** | €3.99 | — | Trial / entry offer |
| 3 Miswak | **€10.99** | €3.66 | €0.98 | Starter Pack |
| 5 Miswak | **€17.49** | €3.50 | €2.46 | **Recommended / Most Popular** |
| 10 Miswak | **€32.99** | €3.30 | €6.91 | Best unit value |

### Merchandising recommendation

The product page should visually prioritize the **3-pack and 5-pack** rather than the single stick.

Recommended labels:
- **1 pack:** “Опитай”
- **3 pack:** “Starter Pack”
- **5 pack:** “Най-популярен”
- **10 pack:** “Най-добра цена”

The **5-pack should be pre-selected by default** on the product page during the launch test.

## Shipping

For launch:

- **1 / 3 / 5 sticks:** shipping is paid by the customer.
- **10 sticks / orders above the free-shipping threshold:** shipping is subsidized by the business.
- Prefer low-cost locker delivery as the default/most visible shipping option where operationally possible.
- Exact shipping charge shown to the customer should be calculated from the current courier contract/integration.

This structure is designed to protect contribution margin while giving customers an incentive to increase basket size.

---

## Free-shipping threshold

**€25**

With the launch pricing, this means the 10-pack qualifies automatically, while 1-, 3-, and 5-packs do not.

Customer-facing message can be:

**“Безплатна доставка за поръчки над €25.”**

or, more product-specifically:

**“Вземи 10 Miswak и получи безплатна доставка.”**

The second version should be A/B tested because it communicates the benefit more concretely.

---

## Payment methods

### Launch default

**Cash on delivery via Postal Money Transfer (Пощенски паричен превод / ППП)** through a licensed courier/postal operator.

Customer-facing checkout wording should remain simple:

**“Наложен платеж — плащате при получаване.”**

The customer does not need to understand or initiate a separate postal transfer; ППП is handled operationally by the courier.

### Optional later addition

**Online card payment**, after the required accounting/fiscal setup is confirmed.

Card payment can improve checkout convenience and reduce refused COD parcels, but its real effect on conversion and failed deliveries should be measured rather than assumed.

## Cost per unit

Product purchase price:

**€1.00 / Miswak**

This assumes the current supplier price and free inbound shipping.

This number must be updated if any of the following become material:
- customs duty;
- import brokerage / customs representation;
- unrecoverable import VAT;
- inspection or certification charges;
- damaged or unusable imported stock.

---

## Packaging cost

Current planning allowance:

**€0.40 / stick**

Includes the current estimated average cost of:
- Bulgarian compliance label;
- brand/logo sticker;
- packaging materials;
- small packing consumables.

### Current all-in physical unit cost

**€1.00 product + €0.40 packaging = €1.40 / sellable stick**

This is the base COGS used in the launch economics below.

---

## Payment processing cost

For conservative financial modelling, keep an allowance equivalent to approximately:

**1.5% of order value + €0.25 per order**

At the assumed launch bundle mix, this equals approximately:

**€0.49 per order on average.**

Important:
- Actual **ППП / COD collection fees** depend on the courier contract.
- If the launch uses ППП only, replace this modelling allowance with the real courier collection fee once the contract is finalized.
- If card payments are added, replace the estimate with the actual processor pricing.

Using the planning bundle mix below:

## Gross margin

Gross margin here means:

**Selling price − product cost − packaging cost**

It excludes advertising, payment/collection fees, shipping subsidy, tax, refunds, and fixed overhead.

| Bundle | Revenue | Physical COGS | Gross profit | Gross margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | €3.99 | €1.40 | €2.59 | **64.9%** |
| 3 | €10.99 | €4.20 | €6.79 | **61.8%** |
| 5 | €17.49 | €7.00 | €10.49 | **60.0%** |
| 10 | €32.99 | €14.00 | €18.99 | **57.6%** |

At the assumed bundle mix:

**Weighted gross margin: ~59.9%**

---

## Contribution margin

Contribution margin here means:

**Revenue − physical COGS − estimated payment processing/collection fee − shipping subsidy**

Advertising is **not** deducted yet because the contribution margin is what is available to pay CAC and then generate profit.

| Bundle | Contribution before ads | Contribution margin |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | **€2.28** | 57.1% |
| 3 | **€6.38** | 58.0% |
| 5 | **€9.98** | 57.0% |
| 10 | **€16.21** | 49.1% |

At the assumed bundle mix:

- **Average contribution before ads: ~€8.70 / order**
- **Weighted contribution margin: ~54.8%**

## Maximum CAC we can afford

### Modelled break-even CAC

**~€8.70 per acquired order**

This is the approximate blended maximum CAC under the assumed bundle mix before the operation starts losing money on the order itself.

Bundle-specific theoretical limits:

| Bundle | Approx. maximum CAC |
|---|---:|
| 1 stick | **€2.28** |
| 3 sticks | **€6.38** |
| 5 sticks | **€9.98** |
| 10 sticks | **€16.21** |

This is why paid acquisition cannot economically depend on single-stick purchases.

### Important rule

**€8.70 is a ceiling, not a target.**

Operating consistently at the ceiling leaves effectively no room for:
- refunds;
- refused COD parcels;
- additional courier charges;
- customer support;
- fixed overhead;
- creative production;
- unexpected losses;
- profit.

---

## Target CAC

### Launch target

**€6–€7 per first order**

Interpretation:

- **≤ €6:** strong / scalable signal
- **€6–€7:** healthy
- **€7–€8.50:** potentially viable, but offer/CRO/creative optimization required
- **€8.50–€10:** too close to or above break-even for comfortable scaling
- **> €10:** do not scale inventory purely because sales are occurring

The CAC target must always be evaluated together with:
- AOV;
- bundle mix;
- conversion rate;
- refused COD rate;
- refund rate;
- real shipping cost.

## Refund / return logic

### Recommended launch policy

1. **Unopened / sealed products**
   - Standard distance-selling withdrawal rules apply.
   - Allow the customer to request withdrawal within the legally applicable 14-day period.

2. **Opened / unsealed Miswak**
   - Because this is an oral-hygiene product, the store should state clearly that the right of withdrawal may not apply once hygienic sealing has been broken, **where the legal hygiene exception is applicable**.
   - The final Terms & Conditions wording should be legally reviewed before launch.

3. **Damaged, defective, mouldy, compromised, or incorrect product**
   - Offer a **replacement or refund**.
   - Ask for a photo where reasonable, but avoid creating disproportionate friction for a €3.99 product.

4. **Change-of-mind return shipping**
   - Customer pays the return shipping where legally permitted and clearly disclosed in advance.

5. **Refund economics**
   - Track refunds and refused COD parcels as a separate KPI from CAC.
   - After the first month, include the observed refund/refusal rate in contribution-margin calculations.

### Legal reference

EU/Bulgarian distance-selling rules generally provide a 14-day withdrawal period for online purchases. EU rules also contain an exception for certain sealed goods that are not suitable for return for health-protection or hygiene reasons after being unsealed.

References:
- European Commission / Your Europe: https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/returns/index_en.htm
- Bulgarian Consumer Protection Commission: https://kzp.bg/bg/potrebiteli/good-to-know/online-shopping
- EUR-Lex consumer-rights summary: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/EN/legal-content/summary/consumer-information-right-of-withdrawal-and-other-consumer-rights.html

## Any guarantee

### Do not launch with a broad “30-day money-back guarantee”

At the current price and validation stage, a broad satisfaction guarantee adds complexity and makes the test data less clean.

Instead use a simple product-condition promise:

### **“Arrives sealed and in good condition — otherwise we replace or refund it.”**

Bulgarian customer-facing version:

**“Ако продуктът пристигне с нарушена опаковка или дефект, ще го заменим или възстановим сумата.”**

This creates trust without making subjective satisfaction claims or encouraging unnecessary returns.

No health, whitening, treatment, or guaranteed-results promise should be attached to the offer.

## Upsell possibilities

### Launch-stage upsells

Prioritize quantity upgrades rather than introducing many new products.

1. **1 → 3 pack upgrade**
   - “Добави още 2 за €7.00”
   - Strongest immediate checkout/order-page upsell.

2. **3 → 5 pack upgrade**
   - Show the incremental cost rather than the full price:
   - “Само +€6.50 за още 2 Miswak.”

3. **5 → 10 pack upgrade**
   - Tie the upgrade to the shipping benefit:
   - “Премини на 10 бр. и получи безплатна доставка.”

4. **Post-purchase replenishment**
   - After real usage data exists, test reorder reminders rather than guessing a replenishment frequency.

### Future upsell / cross-sell possibilities

Only after the core Miswak offer is validated:

- branded Miswak travel/storage holder;
- family pack;
- subscription / recurring replenishment;
- tongue cleaner;
- natural floss;
- complementary oral-care product aligned with the brand positioning.

The first 30-day test should remain focused primarily on **selling Miswak bundles**, not maximizing catalogue size.

## Launch inventory and reorder rule

### Initial inventory

**200 sticks**

Initial inventory cash cost:

- Product: 200 × €1.00 = **€200**
- Packaging: 200 × €0.40 = **€80**
- Total prepared-stock cost: **~€280**

### Reorder decision

Because replenishment takes approximately **20 days**, evaluate performance after the **first 7 days**.

Working decision framework:

| Sticks sold in first 7 days | Interpretation | Inventory action |
|---:|---|---|
| 0–25 | Weak | Do not reorder yet |
| 25–45 | Moderate | Continue testing / reassess within days |
| 45–70 | Good | Reorder approximately 200–250 |
| 70–100 | Very good | Reorder approximately 300–400 |
| 100+ | Strong demand | Consider 400–500, only if CAC economics are healthy |

Do **not** reorder based only on units sold. A reorder should require acceptable CAC and AOV.

## Month-One Success Criteria

Primary operating targets:

- **AOV:** ≥ €14–€15
- **Preferred AOV:** ~€15.87+
- **Target CAC:** €6–€7
- **Hard blended CAC ceiling:** ~€8.70
- Majority of orders should be **3- or 5-stick bundles**
- Paid advertising budget: **€400–€500 maximum**, deployed progressively
- First-week spend should be used to validate creatives, offer, conversion, CAC, and bundle mix before scaling

### Core decision principle

The test is successful only if the product can generate **repeatable contribution after acquisition cost**, not merely sales volume.

---

## Financial-model caveat

All launch-margin figures above are **operational planning figures**, not tax-accounting figures.

They currently do **not** explicitly deduct:
- output VAT if/when the business is registered under the general VAT regime;
- unrecoverable import VAT;
- customs duty;
- customs brokerage;
- accounting costs;
- website/app/platform fees;
- creative-production costs;
- COD refusals;
- chargebacks;
- any courier collection fee not captured by the current payment-cost allowance.

As soon as the actual courier contract, tax treatment, and payment setup are final, the model should be updated before the first paid campaign is scaled.
